Giro di Piazza — OSM Candidate Map

Discovery resource only. This map shows OpenStreetMap candidates whose names begin with Piazza or Piazzetta, including features tagged place=square and named highway=* features. It does not decide whether a square qualifies for the challenge. The written rule still requires the location to be labelled Piazza/Piazzetta on Google Maps and to be named after a qualifying real person. OSM may be incomplete or differently tagged.

Challenge reminder

Find distinct piazze or piazzette in the active NIL that are named after a real person primarily known as a religious figure, politician/activist, artist/cultural figure, scientist/engineer, or military leader.
