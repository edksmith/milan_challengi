Fiat Panda Finder — Reference Guide

Use this guide to help identify qualifying Fiat Pandas and their colour categories for the Fiat Finder challenge.

The goal is not to identify the exact model year or official Fiat paint code. If the vehicle is clearly a Fiat Panda and clearly belongs to one of the five qualifying colour families below, it counts.

1. What does a Fiat Panda look like?

Fiat Pandas have changed considerably over the years. Older and newer generations all count.

2. Qualifying Colours

The five challenge colours use current Fiat-style Italian names for fun. The car does not need to have been sold under that exact official paint name. Older Panda paint colours count as long as the vehicle is clearly within the appropriate colour family.

⚫ Nero Cinema — BLACK

All clearly black Pandas count. Does not count: dark grey or silver.

🔴 Rosso Passione — RED

All clearly red Pandas count, including darker shades that are unmistakably red. Does not count: orange.

🔵 Blu Italia — BLUE

All clearly blue Pandas count. Different light and dark shades of blue are acceptable. If the colour is ambiguous blue/green, ask the group chat to confirm.

🟢 Verde Foresta — GREEN

All clearly green Pandas count. If the colour is ambiguous blue/green, ask the group chat to confirm.

🟡 Giallo Positano — YELLOW

All clearly yellow Pandas count. Does not count: orange, beige or gold.

3. General Rules

  • The vehicle must be a genuine Fiat Panda.
  • The colour is judged by the vehicle's visible overall body colour, not the official Fiat paint name.
  • Wraps or repaints count if the visible colour clearly qualifies.
  • A Panda may count for only one colour.
  • Two-tone cars count according to the predominant body colour.
  • If a colour is genuinely ambiguous, send a photo to the group chat for a Game Master ruling.
  • Do not touch the vehicle.
  • Take photographs respectfully and avoid deliberately photographing licence plates or people.

Spirit of the challenge: If a reasonable person would clearly describe the Panda as black, red, blue, green or yellow, it should normally count.
