Milanese Derby — Reference Guide

Use this guide to help identify qualifying symbols and iconography for Milan's two main football clubs: AC Milan and Inter Milan.

What counts?

An item counts if it clearly supports or identifies the chosen club. It may use the official crest, club name, nickname, colours, recognizable club-specific imagery, or branded merchandise.

The same physical location may count only once, regardless of how many qualifying items are present there.

Generic red-and-black or blue-and-black objects do not count unless there is some additional indication that they refer to AC Milan or Inter.
